Chan Lee

I'm a Robotics Engineer and former robotics startup founder. I started a warehouse robotics company that was acquired by Neuromeka, a listed Korean robotics company. I'm now on its AI team, mentored by Joonho Lee. Since September 2026, I've also been a visiting researcher in Prof. Sehoon Ha's lab at Georgia Tech's School of Interactive Computing. My research there centers on implicit spatiotemporal (4D) world models for humanoid locomotion with Fauna Sprout, and on quantifying their uncertainty so that a robot can decide and act robustly when much of the world is occluded or changing. This work is supported by Amazon Robotics. I received my BS in Mechanical Engineering from KAIST. I was selected for the Forbes 30 Under 30 Asia 2024.
